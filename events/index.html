Alchemy Comedy Theater

al·che·my [ˈal-kə-mē]
a process of transforming the common into the special

Since 2011, the Alchemy Comedy Theater has been Greenville’s best combination of weekly improv, stand up, and sketch comedy shows. These live energetic shows feature different local performers and special guests each week.

All shows are Thursday ($10), Friday ($15), and Saturday ($15) nights at 7pm and 8:30pm in Greenville’s iconic Coffee Underground theater. Tickets can be purchased at the door with cash or credit, or via website (alchemycomedy.com). Street parking is available as well as nearby parking garages.

Alchemy Comedy also has improvisation classes starting all the time. For details about upcoming classes and online registration check out their classes page. More questions? Contact Alchemy producer at: harrison@greenvilleimprov.com or by phone at 864-256-1467.
