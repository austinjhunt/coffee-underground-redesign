HOT coffee. COOL vibe.

We are a local, native to Greenville, coffee house & cafe, open since 1995...

specializing in homemade desserts, delicious hot, iced or frozen coffees using our house-roasted FTO coffee beans, hot chocolates & teas (including matcha) plus fruit smoothies and all-day breakfast & lunch.

See the menu Directions

Located at the corner of Coffee & Main, nestled 12 steps down from Ristorante Bergamo (like Cheers).

THEATER underground

Our theater room has been a cultural hotspot since 1995! Each week, you can see stand up, sketch & Alchemy Improv Comedy, plus local songwriters & Wits End Poetry. In addition to weekly shows, we are hosts to independent film screenings & performers from all around.

SOME DAYS THE COFFEE MAKES YOU.

Something for everyone...

in addition to our exceptional coffees and homemade desserts, our food menu offers all-day breakfast, salads, sandwiches, burgers, cheesy fries & other appetizers, plus power smoothies, the black tiger milkshake, beer, wine & cocktails, including our espresso martini.

Plenty of comfy seating...for meeting family, friends & colleagues. We also have a meeting room that seats up to 30 guests, where we offer catering services for business lunches, breakfast meetings, baby & bridal showers & all kinds of parties.

Looking for Mice on Main? Marvin Jr. is a regular, you can't miss him at the top of our steps!
